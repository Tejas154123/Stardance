About StarDance

Stardance is a fiction, one of a kind dance competition that brings together performers from all styles and backgronds to compete under the stars. From contemporary to hip hop , every dancer gets a chance to shine on a stage unlike any other.

Solo Showdown

Dance crews bring high energy group routines for the biggest prize of the night.
