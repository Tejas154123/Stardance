About StarDance

Stardance is a fiction, one of a kind dance competition that brings together performers from all styles and backgronds to compete under the stars. From contemporary to hip hop , every dancer gets a chance to shine on a stage unlike any other.

Events

Solo Showdown

SOLO-Individual dancers take the stage to showcase their unique style of dancing in front of the judges.

Duo Battle

DUO-pairs go head to head , blending choreography and chemistry to win over the crowd.

Crew Championship

CREW-Dance crews bring high energy group routines for the biggest prize of the night.
